## GPA Calculator Compass

**Website:** [gpacalculator.website](https://gpacalculator.website/)

**Category:** Education · GPA guides · Academic planning  
**Language:** English

### What it offers

GPA Calculator Compass explains GPA calculations, credit weighting, and grading-scale differences. Its homepage currently presents an educational guide rather than an interactive calculator form. Listed reading topics include cumulative, weighted, unweighted, high-school, percentage-based, and final-exam GPA questions.

The guide distinguishes the arithmetic from institutional rules. It discusses how repeated courses, transfer credits, and different credit systems can complicate an estimate. That distinction is the main reason for including it here.

### Why we recommend exploring it

- **It encourages understanding before calculation.** A number is easier to evaluate when you can explain the assumptions behind it. We prefer that learning-first approach to treating an unexplained result as authoritative.
- **It gives readers a useful starting question.** Before comparing results, ask whether the same courses, credits, and grading scale were used. This is a productive habit to develop alongside any calculator.
- **It supports more purposeful reading.** Choose a topic that matches your question instead of reading every guide indiscriminately. We recommend starting with terminology, then moving to the calculation relevant to your situation.

### A suggested way to use it

Read the homepage, write down the grading rules you still need to confirm, and compare those questions with your institution's published policy. Keep a small worksheet documenting your inputs so future estimates remain comparable.

### Important limits

This recommendation concerns the public educational material, not independently tested calculator software. School-specific policies and official records take priority. Do not treat a third-party guide as an official transcript, a universal conversion standard, or a guarantee of an admissions outcome.

**Start here:** [Read the GPA guide](https://gpacalculator.website/).

## Terraria Wiki Fieldbook

**Website:** [terrariawiki.site](https://terrariawiki.site/)

**Category:** Gaming · Terraria guides · Fan references  
**Language:** English

### What it offers

Terraria Wiki Fieldbook is an independent fan-made reference site, not an official Terraria resource or a site affiliated with the game's developers. Its homepage covers crafting, boss preparation, loot research, and checking version differences. Listed article topics include classes, housing, NPCs, fishing rods, wings, potions, Night's Edge, and mods such as Calamity.

The site's emphasis is on asking a specific gameplay question and checking the conditions behind an answer, rather than treating every tip as universally applicable.

### Why we recommend exploring it

- **Its organization matches real gameplay interruptions.** We like resources that help turn a vague feeling of being stuck into a concrete research task. A focused question is easier to investigate than an entire progression route at once.
- **It encourages verification rather than blind copying.** The homepage asks readers to consider platform and version differences. We consider this a valuable reading habit, especially before spending substantial time on a build.
- **It offers several topical entry points.** A player can choose an equipment, housing, or progression topic according to the current problem. Our recommendation is to use those entry points selectively, not as a mandatory checklist.

### A suggested way to use it

Write down your current objective and the uncertainty blocking it. Read the relevant guide, then compare the advice with your game version and an authoritative source. Test a small change before committing to a larger project. Keep vanilla-game research separate from mod-specific guidance.

### Important limits

This is a discovery recommendation, not a claim that every recipe, statistic, or strategy has been independently verified. We do not present the site as exhaustive or guaranteed current. For consequential gameplay details, consult official patch notes and check the behavior in your own game.

**Start here:** [Explore the Terraria fieldbook](https://terrariawiki.site/).

## Crescent Solitaire

**Website:** [crescentsolitaire.online](https://crescentsolitaire.online/)

**Category:** Browser games · Solitaire · Card strategy  
**Language:** English

### What it offers

Crescent Solitaire presents a two-deck patience game in the browser. Its published rules describe 104 cards, sixteen fans, and eight foundations: four build upward from aces and four downward from kings, by suit. Unlike Klondike, moves between fans use the same suit and can build in either direction.

The homepage advertises free play without registration or a download, visible cards throughout each fan, a shuffle preview, unlimited undo, and device-local progress saving. It also describes Classic, Relaxed, and Easy settings with three, six, and nine shuffles respectively, alongside daily and numbered deals.

### Why we recommend exploring it

- **A distinctive alternative to familiar solitaire.** Two opposing foundation directions give readers a different puzzle to explore rather than another copy of the usual seven-column layout.
- **An emphasis on informed decisions.** The described shuffle preview is especially appealing: it lets a player inspect the consequences before committing a limited resource. In this ruleset a shuffle rotates cards within fans rather than randomly mixing them.
- **Room to learn through experimentation.** Unlimited undo, including shuffles according to the site, is a useful advertised feature for comparing alternative moves without immediately abandoning a deal.
- **Rules available beside the game.** The homepage explains legal moves, empty-fan restrictions, controls, and strategic considerations. We appreciate that a newcomer can read the relevant rules before diving into a less familiar variant.

### A suggested way to use it

Read the rules, start a deal, and inspect both foundation directions before moving cards. Use the preview to ask which cards a shuffle would expose. Prefer a more forgiving setting while learning, and treat undo as a way to understand a decision rather than simply chase a result.

### Important limits

This introduction is based on the public homepage and describes advertised features; it is not an end-to-end gameplay, mobile compatibility, or save-restoration test. The site says progress is stored locally, so do not assume cross-device synchronization or recovery after clearing browser data. We have not independently verified its solvable-deal claims, and inclusion does not imply that every numbered deal can be won.

**Getting started:** Open the website linked above and read the rules before your first deal.
